NOT CAPTURED

ItemReason
Activity severity written by the office; real Google Calendar eventsThe office stores no event severity today and no Google Calendar is connected in the replica. The "New in this release" Activity and Schedule images use fixture records with a severity and two appended Google events, added at the browser boundary, to show how the page draws them. They prove the page, not the office.
Thumbnails of documents held in a connected source (Drive) and read on demandCovered by the document-preview browser suite with a stubbed gateway; not drawn here because the replica holds no connected source. Uploaded PDFs and pictures are shown.
Real iOS Safari (any screen)No WebKit or iOS device in the build environment. Every "390x844" image is Chromium with touch emulation, not iOS Safari.
Real microphone, dictation and live voice in useNo audio hardware; only the buttons and idle states are shown. Voice is covered by simulated-audio suites, not by these images.
Hosted preview signed in / Sample Office on the hosted previewThe hosted preview cannot sign in (its API base is a placeholder); that is why this gallery exists. Every image here is a local capture.
Real Grant office data, real Google calendar, real Telegram/SlackProduction access is forbidden for this task. Data is synthetic rows in an isolated replica.
The Attention pageIt did not open in the capture lab, which has no waiting items; the Attention count is visible in the top bar.
Module colour menu, Layouts menu and sticky notes on a phoneNot offered on a phone by design (the arrangement controls are off there and the modules stack). Desktop captures included.
Calendar Day / Week / Month on the empty stand-in officeShown on the populated replica only.
Full-page / long-scroll capturesTeam HQ is a fixed-viewport canvas (ADR-0072): the page itself does not scroll, lists scroll inside their module or sheet. Captures are viewport images; lists are shown through the maximised module views. Settings panes taller than a phone screen are cut at the viewport.
Operating-system push notificationsNot part of the page. The in-page undo bar and dialogs are captured.
Other looks and accent colours, other screen sizesThis gallery is the Original look at two sizes only (1440x900 and 390x844), Light and Dark, as asked. The earlier gallery had the other looks and sizes.
