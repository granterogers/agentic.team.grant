# Two independent adversarial reviews — findings and reconciliation

> Two fresh reviewers were given only the brief's prompts and read-only access (code, and `select`-only production
> SQL). Neither saw my reasoning. **Every finding below was either verified against the code/database by me or marked
> as not verified.** Where a reviewer was wrong, that is recorded with the evidence; where I was wrong, so is that.
> The documents in this folder were amended to match; this file is the log of why.

reviewer's proposed minimum before any second office exists: F1, F2, F3, F7, F9, then F4–F6. **All accepted.**

### Claims in my documents that Review A found false — all corrected

1. "Production job creation goes through five SQL functions" → **twelve** (F7).
2. "Only `service_role`-callable `app.act_as` can set the office GUC" → false; `act_as` removed (F11).
3. "No table has two routes to two different offices" → false for `relations`, `branch_groups`, `consultations` (F8).
4. "The 152 gateway functions are the surface" → the inline gateway paths are outside the map (F1, F12).
5. "`offices.kind` is immutable" → no enforcement existed; trigger added (F11).
6. "Purge by `office_id` is complete" → ledgers lack the column; purge **removed** (C-S6).
7. "Sample is truthfully disconnected" → it was only a display row while credentials resolved globally (F1).
8. "Sessions carry a confirmed email" (a gateway comment) → `email_confirmed_at` is never checked; irrelevant once binding is by auth id.
9. "An old call cannot resolve a `p_office`-first overload" — true but beside the point: the old overload stays callable until the contract. Overloads are now limited to the worker's hot path (T3).
10. "NOT NULL, no system office" vs "nullable-by-design" → resolved by the `system` office (F7).

## What the two reviews agree is necessary (the core that survives)

`offices` with its own key plus `kind`/`state`; composite FKs and an immutable `office_id` on the carriers (the one
guard that holds for the service role); a **required** `p_office` with no default on reachable functions;
per-office uniqueness; the canary test with its positive control; `may_act_outside` joined to `offices.kind`; channel
identity → office with no `or subject is null`; the E1/E2 hotfixes; the backfill with abort-on-unknown and rollback.

## Consequence for the readiness verdict

Review A found no flaw in the *shape* of the design — it found places where my documents contradicted themselves,
under-counted a surface, or let a derivation stand where an explicit check was needed, and **two present-day
vulnerabilities (F1, F2) that exist with one office** and that the design had wrongly allowed to wait. Both are now
the first things built. None of the findings required a different ownership primitive.
