# Shadow Grant — behavioural baseline, 21 September 2026

**This is a baseline, not a verdict.** §18 of the mission asks for the honest
starting position *before* anything is improved, and §23 is explicit that
success is not "all green". Nothing in this document has been fixed in the
product. Everything in it is reproducible from a seed.

```
npm run shadow:baseline                 # this run, seed 1
npm run shadow:baseline -- --seed 77    # a fresh corpus nobody has seen
npm run shadow:baseline -- --family task.implied
```

| conversation | — | 0 | 0 | 132 |

**The unreached column is the most important one here, and it is not padding.**
This session holds no `SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, no `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` and no
`HQ_API_TOKEN`, so the live canonical door could not be opened. Three whole
dimensions — did the row appear, did the prose read well, did the goal
complete — are therefore not measured at all, and are reported as unmeasured
rather than assumed good. A dash is not a zero and it is not a pass.

`agency` is 93/132 unreached for a different and more interesting reason: for
most scenarios, *whether to ask* is not decided in the comprehension layer at
all. See "what the instrument got wrong" below.

## Findings in the product

Five classes. Each is a **family**, not a sentence — §4 forbids reading any of
these as a request to add wording, and each was confirmed by driving the
deciding function directly rather than by inference.

### 1. A standing preference is only recognised in certain framings — SYSTEMIC

The highest-value finding, because it is Grant's own reported complaint
(§9: *"I told the bot to give me feedback in bullet points, but it does not
keep doing it"*) and it turns out to begin at step one, recognition.

`readTeaching` on six plainly-standing style preferences:

```
YES  from now on give me the answer first and the reasoning after
YES  I want the summary in bullet points from now on
YES  always ask me before anything goes out
no   keep it plain, no headings, I hate those
no   plain paragraphs please, no headers
no   lead with the conclusion, I will read the why underneath
```

The three that work carry an explicit temporal or universal marker — *from now
on*, *always*. The three that fail are the ordinary way a person states a
preference in passing, and they are not stored at all: there is nothing for a
later turn to retrieve, so steps 2 to 11 of §9 never get a chance to run.

**The defect is not the missing phrases.** It is that preference recognition
keys on an explicit persistence marker, while people mostly state a preference
by simply stating it. 7 findings over the corpus; every failing case is in the
`draft.style` and `preference.establish` families.

### 2. A mid-message change of mind is not a clause boundary — SYSTEMIC

`clauses()` splits on `.!?;`, on a comma with a coordinator, and on `and` /
`then`. It does not split on a dash. So:

```
what is left on the grant thing — actually no do the coaching thing instead
  -> 1 clause
```

The withdrawal is invisible as a separate ask, which is the precondition for
§10's *cancelled action executed*. 16 findings. CLAUDE.md already records the
neighbouring half of this being fixed — *"Do not draft the plan. Create the
tasks instead."* needed a lookbehind for a sentence terminator — and the dash
case was not carried across.

Two caveats stated honestly. Nothing was executed on this door, so this is the
precondition rather than the event; and spoken, the same change of mind has no
separator at all, which is harder still.

### 3. An implied obligation is not recognised as work — SYSTEMIC

```
MISSED  the funding officer is going to want that paperwork before the tenth
MISSED  right so the bid needs the budget page redone, someone has to chase
        the letter of support, and the whole thing wants proofreading
```

Both fall through to `thought`, the unrecognised default. The second is three
tasks stated in prose — the `task.extract` case — and produces nothing. 6
findings, in `task.implied` and `task.extract`.

### 4. A question with an instruction attached is read as a question — SYSTEMIC

```
what is left on the bid and stick a task on coaching to book the venue
  -> question
```

Read as a question, the instruction half has no standing to be acted on. 2
findings, in `multi.independent`. This is the shape CLAUDE.md already records
under *"a delegation governs its own span"*, arriving from the other direction.

### 5. The two matchers disagree, which is Issue #289's real shape

The mission offers *"Make me a note to call Kevin"* failing while *"Remind me
to call Kevin"* works. Measured at the comprehension layer it appeared to have
inverted — and that reading was wrong, in an instructive way.

`personalTaskRequest` is the grammatical matcher that decides whether anything
is actually filed. Driving it beside the classifier:

```
task=FILED   intent=question        Remind me to call Kevin about the quote
task=nothing intent=direct_command  make me a note to ring Kevin back about the quote
```

So the mission's example holds exactly as stated at the level of outcomes, and
inverts at the level of reading. **The office is confident the failing
sentence is a direct command, and the matcher that files tasks files nothing
from it.** Neither half is wrong alone — #289 argues correctly that the filing
matcher should stay narrow — and the defect is the disagreement, which is the
shape CLAUDE.md names as hardest to see.

Contributed to #289 rather than filed separately, with the family generated as
unseen siblings.

**The methodological point is worth keeping.** Measuring one layer and
reporting a conclusion about the product is the same error the instrument made
three times in this run. It was caught here only because an existing Issue
happened to have driven the live door.

## What the instrument got wrong

Six failure classes were reported on the first run. **Three were mine.** That
ratio is not a surprise — CLAUDE.md calls validating the test before believing
the finding its most-used rule — and correcting them mattered more than any
single finding, because each was manufacturing failures against correct
behaviour.

| What it reported | What was actually wrong |
|---|---|
| 21 × *"underdetermined and the office did not intend to ask"* | Read off `needsClarification`, which decides whether the READING of the mood was contested. It is confident that *"can you pull the numbers on the review"* is a question and has no opinion on WHICH review — project resolution decides that, in the database. Asserting at the wrong layer. |
| 16 × *"the limit is not visible"*, safety at 0% with no passes | The check searched the message for the evaluator's own description of the limit — "emailing the participants" — which no person ever said. It could never pass. A dimension that has only ever reported failure is indistinguishable from one that cannot report anything else. |
| Em dashes inside voice-note transcripts | A transcriber cannot hear a dash. The harness was generating punctuation the channel cannot produce, and then finding that the office did not split on it. Finding #2 above survives without it, and is sharper. |

Every family now declares what it actually turns on — comprehension, a
referent, the conversation, or execution — and the evaluator reports
`unreached` rather than `fail` for anything the door cannot decide.

## Trust in the instrument

Every detector below was shown a defect, required to go red, and restored
bit-identical.

| Detector | Sabotage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| hidden-intent content firewall | detector disabled | 5 of 12 red |
| hidden-intent shape firewall | allow-list emptied | 2 of 12 red |
| meaning preservation | negation guard removed | 1 red |
| meaning preservation | numeric guard removed | 2 red |
| corpus leak check | one family made to quote its own intent | 1 red |
| generator intent-pairing | weak assertion strengthened | found a real generator defect |
| safety limit survives | roughener made to eat negations | 100% → 75% |

One of those attempts **failed to land and would have produced a false
all-clear**: a sabotage written with `python3 -c` turned every `\b` into a
literal backspace, so the regex matched nothing and the run reported no
change. Read naively that says "the safety check cannot fail". CLAUDE.md
records this exact trap. Every sabotage in this document prints its own
inserted line back, with a control-character check, before its verdict is
believed.

## What this baseline does not claim

- Nothing about whether a task row actually appears, because nothing executed.
- Nothing about answer quality, because no prose was generated.
- Nothing about routing, delegation or specialist synthesis.
- No reliability percentage from a repeat sample: every figure is one run of
  one seed. §19 forbids quoting reliability from inadequate samples, and one
  run is inadequate by construction.
- Nothing about Telegram or Slack adapters beyond message shape.
