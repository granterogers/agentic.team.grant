# Real-world failure intake

Things the office did in normal use that were wrong, confusing or misleading.
Added with `npm run shadow:intake`, which takes about two minutes and asks in
Grant's own vocabulary.

**Each one becomes a family, never a phrase.** The original transcript is kept
here; what goes into the corpus is the shape, with siblings that share none of
the words. §4 and CLAUDE.md's standing rule are the same instruction, and the
intake exists to make obeying it cheaper than not.
