## Approvals required (each is a separate, narrow yes)

1. **Apply migrations 1023 and 1024** to production (`ALLOW_PRODUCTION_WRITES=1`, `migrate:one`, two files).
2. **Deploy the modified `hq-api` gateway** (`deploy:hq-api`, one function).
3. **Deploy the page** to the production address (`deploy:hq-page`). This is not a merge to main; main is untouched until Grant says so.

previous save; a gateway before its migration answers 503 for backgrounds and the page keeps the picture in the browser).

## Acceptance (what "deployed and verified" means here)

- #442: two real devices, one project, a Post-it added on each within a second: both present on both after a minute; a deletion on one is not undone by the other;
  `select count(*) from public.dashboard_layouts` unchanged by the deploy; the page with the gateway rolled back still saves (fallback).
- #445: a picture uploaded on device A is drawn on device B without uploading; Remove on A shows the default on B after its next refresh; a second office cannot read
  it (`project_background` for A's slug answers `null`); a Sample visitor cannot upload; the thirteenth picture in an office is refused.

## Stored pictures after a rollback

Pictures stay in the private `files` bucket under `<office_id>/backgrounds/` and are unreachable once the gateway is rolled back. To delete them, list that prefix in
the Supabase Storage dashboard (or `POST /storage/v1/object/list/files`) and remove the objects; nothing else references them.
