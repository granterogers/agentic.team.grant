Service marks in this directory
===============================

These are simplified, self-drawn SVG marks identifying the services this office
connects to. They are NOT the vendors' official brand assets.

Why drawn rather than downloaded:

  1. Official brand kits are licensed for identification but carry conditions on
     colour, clear space and modification that a monochrome/disabled state in a
     settings list would breach.
  2. Remote assets do not load in the capture sandbox, so every screenshot would
     be judged with them missing.
  3. Each vendor's SVG arrives at a different weight, grid and optical size, so
     a row of eight looks like eight logos rather than one list.

Each mark is redrawn on a common 24x24 grid at a common stroke weight, using
`currentColor` so it can take the connected / degraded / off states. They are
recognisable identifications, used nominatively to say which service a
connection is to — not reproductions of the trademarks.

If a vendor's brand guidelines require their official asset, replace the mark
here and record it below with a link to the guidelines and the terms.
