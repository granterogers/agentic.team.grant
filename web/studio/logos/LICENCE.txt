Service marks in this directory
===============================

Most of these marks are the services' own logos, taken from Simple Icons
(version 16.35.0, https://simpleicons.org). Simple Icons publishes the artwork
under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The
trademarks in each mark still belong to the company that owns them. They are
used only to say which service a connection is to, which is a nominative use.

Taken from Simple Icons, one filled path each, on a 24x24 grid: Notion, Google
Drive, Google Calendar, Gmail, NotebookLM, Telegram, GitHub, OpenRouter,
Supabase and Zoom. Each company's own brand page has its usage rules.

Drawn by hand, because Simple Icons no longer publishes it:

  Slack         four-lozenge mark, drawn. Replace it with Slack's own artwork
                from its brand guidelines page when it is taken.

Generic glyphs, deliberately not a company's mark (browser, MCP client, direct
upload, voice, email).

If a vendor's brand guidelines require a different asset, replace the mark in
marks.js and update the entry above. All trademarks are the property of their
respective owners.
