Limited Use

Team HQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features described on this page. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to build profiles of you for anyone else. People do not read your Google data unless you ask us to, or it is needed for security or to comply with law.

Stopping access

You can stop Team HQ at any time:

  • In Team HQ, go to Settings → Connections → Google and choose Disconnect.
  • Or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who else sees your data

We share data only with the providers listed above, and only as needed to run the features you use. We do not sell it. We do not share it with advertisers.
