What Grant is judging (§13 — cannot be automated)
  • Naturalness — does it feel like talking to someone, or like dictating to a machine?
  • Latency — the number above is measured; what matters is whether it feels like a pause.
  • Interruption — talk over it mid-sentence. Does it stop cleanly, or talk through you?
  • Echo — try it once on speaker. Does it hear itself? (Listed UNVERIFIED in the spec.)
  • Voice quality — pleasant for 20 minutes, or grating after two?
  • 20+ minutes is the requirement, and it cannot be delegated.
